Premium Notes v1.0.0

A simple yet powerful note-taking app designed for speed and privacy.

How to backup your notes:

  1. Click "Export Backup" to download your notes.
  2. Save the notes-backup.json file in a safe place.
  3. To restore, use the "Import Backup" button and select your file.
